# Product: Premier Siding & Roofing Specialist LLC website

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages. Cal Sans from Google Fonts is the only external resource.
- **Surface mode:** Persuade (one-page site for a local trade business)

## Users
- Homeowners who need siding or roofing work and find the business through Facebook (the business advertises by cross-posting a flyer to New Jersey contractor groups). They open the link on a phone, often inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner, who gets the site for free; it has to look like their real business, done better than they'd expect, on the first phone screen.
- Customer age: unknown (not flagged as 40+ in the brief), so the default calm style applies.

## Purpose
Turn a Facebook flyer into a real web presence: show what the business does, make it easy to call or text for a quote, and look trustworthy without inventing proof.

## Positioning
A siding and roofing contractor whose own line is "Our goal is your satisfaction!!" (flyer, 2026-10-21). Trust has to come from tone and clarity, because there are no reviews, photos of work, licence number or years in business on record.
